一个德布罗意波长为\(\lambda_1\)的中子和另一个德布罗意波长为\(\lambda_2\)的氘核同向正碰后结合成一个氚核，该氚核的德布罗意波长为（不考虑产生光子的动量）\paren[A]
\begin{choices}
	\item \(\dfrac{\lambda_1\lambda_2}{\lambda_1 +\lambda_2}\)
	\item \(\dfrac{\lambda_1\lambda_2}{\lambda_1 -\lambda_2}\)
	\item \(\dfrac{\lambda_1 +\lambda_2}{2}\)
	\item \(\dfrac{\lambda_1 -\lambda_2}{2}\)
\end{choices}
\begin{answer}
    A\qquad 提示：碰撞动量守恒\(\frac{h}{\lambda_1} + \frac{h}{\lambda_2} = \frac{h}{\lambda_3}\)
\end{answer}
