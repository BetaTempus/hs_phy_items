如图甲所示，质量均为\(m\)的四个小球\(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\)、\(D\)通过四根同样长度的轻绳连接并悬挂于\(O\)点，现对\(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\)、\(D\)四球分别施加水平方向的力\(F_1\)、\(F_2\)、\(F_3\)、\(F_4\)，四球静止时如图乙所示，轻绳\(OA\)、\(BC\)、\(CD\)与竖直方向成\(\theta =\)\,\qty{45}{\degree}，轻绳\(AB\)竖直，求\(F_1\)、\(F_2\)、\(F_3\)、\(F_4\)大小和方向。

\begin{tikzpicture}[font=\scriptsize]
	\begin{scope}
		\foreach \x in {-.6,-.4,...,.4}
			\draw[gray] (\x,.2)--++(.2,-.2);
		\draw[thick] (-.6,0)--(.6,0);
		\node at(-.2,-.2){\(O\)};	
		\draw[magenta] (0,0)--(0,-4);
		\foreach \y/\c in {-1/A,-2/B,-3/C,-4/D}
			\shade[ball color=gray] (0,\y)node[right]{\(\c\)} circle(.1);
		\node at(0,-4.2)[below]{ 甲};
	\end{scope}
	\begin{scope}[xshift=3cm]
		\foreach \x in {-.6,-.4,...,.4}
			\draw[gray] (\x,.2)--++(.2,-.2);

		\node at(-.2,-.2){\(O\)};
		\draw[magenta] (0,0)--(-45:1)coordinate(A)--++(-90:1)coordinate(B)--++(-45:1)coordinate(C)--++(-135:1)coordinate(D);
		\draw[dashed,cyan] (0,0)--(0,-3.5) (B)--(B|-0,-3.5);

			\shade[ball color=gray] (\X)node[right]{\(\X\)} circle(.1);
		\draw (-45:.3) arc(-45:-90:.3) ({sqrt(2)/2},{-1.3-sqrt(2)/2}) arc(-90:-45:.3) ({sqrt(2)/2},{-1.5*sqrt(2)-.7}) arc(90:45:.3);

\begin{answer}
    \(F_1 = 4mg\) 向右；\(F_2 = 2mg\)向左；\(F_3 = 3mg\)向右；\(F_4 = mg\)向左
\end{answer}
