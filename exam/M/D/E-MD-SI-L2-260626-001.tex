声音在空气中的传播速度\(v\)与空气的密度\(\rho\)、压强\(p\)有关，下列速度表达式中\(K\)为比例系数，无单位，则这四个表达式中可能正确的是\paren[B]
\begin{choices}
    \item \(v = \frac{Kp}{\rho}\)
    \item \(v = \sqrt{\frac{Kp}{\rho}}\)
    \item \(v = \sqrt{\frac{K\rho}{p}}\)
    \item \(v = \sqrt{Kp\rho}\)
\end{choices}
\begin{answer}
    B
\end{answer}
