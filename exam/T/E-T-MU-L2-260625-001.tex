汽车内燃机利用奥托循环进行工作，奥托循环由两条绝热线和两条等容线组成，其中\(a\)到\(b\)和\(c\)到\(d\)为绝热过程，\(b\)到\(c\)和\(d\)到\(a\)为等容过程，下列说法正确的是\paren[]

\begin{tikzpicture}[decoration={markings,mark=at position 0.5 with {\arrow{>}}},font=\scriptsize,scale=.9]
    \draw[->,blue] (0,0)--(0,3.5)node[right]{\(p\)};
    \draw[->,blue] (0,0)--(3.5,0)node[below]{\(V\)};
    \draw[postaction={decorate},purple] (.6,1.2)node[below]{\(b\)}--(.6,2.7)node[right]{\(c\)};
    \draw[postaction={decorate},purple] (2.6,1.3)node[above]{\(d\)}--(2.6,.5)node[below]{\(a\)};
    \draw[postaction={decorate},purple] (.6,2.7)..controls(1,1.8)and(2,1.4)..(2.6,1.3);
    \draw[postaction={decorate},purple] (2.6,.5)..controls(2,.5)and(1,.9)..(.6,1.2);
    \node at(-.2,-.2)[blue]{\(O\)};
\end{tikzpicture}
\begin{choices}
    \item \(a\to b\)过程中，外界对气体做功
    \item \(b\to c\)过程中，气体分子的平均动能不变
    \item \(c\to a\)过程中，单位体积内气体分子数减小
    \item \(d\to a\)过程中，气体从外界吸收热量
\end{choices}
